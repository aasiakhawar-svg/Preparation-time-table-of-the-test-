Monday 9 November: assessment day

Before the assessment: 20 minutes only. Read the reagent sheet and redraw two mechanisms. Don’t start new topics.
In the assessment: allow about 1 minute per mark, answer every question, show working, and use the whole space provided for “explain” questions.

Marks-winning habits

  • Always give reagent and conditions (for example “reflux with acidified K₂Cr₂O₇”).
  • Curly arrows start at a lone pair or bond, and end on an atom or bond.
  • Quote observations, not only names: colour changes, precipitates.
  • Show every calculation step and include units.
